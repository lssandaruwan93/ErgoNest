Important

ErgoNest does not currently own or physically test every product discussed. Reviews are compiled from manufacturer specifications, published documentation and publicly available information. Where a product has not been handled first-hand, this is stated clearly in the article.

Our evaluation criteria

Every review is structured around the same questions, so you can compare across products without re-learning a new format each time:

  • Adjustability. Which dimensions can be changed, and over what range?
  • Fit for different bodies. Who is this likely to suit, and who may find it limiting?
  • Build and materials. What the manufacturer specifies, and what that implies for longevity.
  • Space and installation. Footprint, assembly complexity, and whether it suits small rooms.
  • Considerations and trade-offs. What you give up, not just what you gain.
  • Value in context. How it compares to alternatives at a similar level, without inventing prices.

Note

We do not publish star ratings, "best of" awards or performance scores unless they are backed by a documented, reproducible method.
